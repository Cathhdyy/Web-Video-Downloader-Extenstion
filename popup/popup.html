No Video Detected? 🕵️

Try these quick steps to help MediaGrabber detect the video stream:

1
Click Play: Most streaming websites only load video segments after you click the play button.
2
Unmute / Volume: Unmute the player or adjust volume slightly to trigger the media decoder.
3
Switch Quality: Changing resolution (e.g. 720p/1080p) inside the player forces an M3U8 manifest request.

MediaGrabber PRO Help 💡

⚡ Instant Features:
  • Protocol Badge: Shows whether a stream is HLS (transmuxed to MP4) or direct HTTP.
  • Quality Dropdown: Select preferred resolution (1080p, 720p, etc.) before clicking Download.
  • Quick Rename: Click the pencil ✏️ or click directly on the filename to rename before downloading.
  • Dismiss Card: Click ✕ on the top-right of any video to remove it from the list.
  • Audio Extraction: Use the dropdown arrow on Download to extract pure MP3 audio.
